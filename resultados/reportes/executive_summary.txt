EXECUTIVE SUMMARY - GENDER AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT ANALYSIS
======================================================================
Updated: 2026-10-20 (revision of manuscript socsci-4597945, MDPI Social Sciences)

NOTE ON THIS VERSION:
The previous version of this file (generated 2025-07-25) came from an
earlier dataset of 63 countries that still included India. India was
removed from the analytical sample; DATA_GHAB.xlsx contains 62 countries.
That version also reported R² from a single 70/30 train-test split with
predictor screening done on the full sample. Those figures are superseded
by the leakage-free results below (script: robustness/code_S1_robustness.py).

DATASET INFORMATION:
- Number of countries analyzed: 62 (low- and lower-middle-income)
- Predictors: 128 gender-related World Bank indicators
- Target variable: G_GPD_PCAP_SLOPE (OLS slope of GDP per capita,
  current US$, on calendar year; available years 1960-2023)
- Missing values: none in the final dataset; no values were imputed

TOP 10 VARIABLES MOST CORRELATED WITH THE GDP PER CAPITA TREND (Pearson):

 2. Death by communicable/maternal conditions, F 15-59        r =  0.470
 3. Death by injury, F 15-59                                  r = -0.460
 4. Death by communicable/maternal conditions, F 60+          r =  0.445
 5. Fertility rate, total                                     r = -0.418
 6. Prevalence of obesity, female 18+                         r =  0.414
 7. Death by injury, female                                   r = -0.385
 8. Literacy rate, youth, gender parity index                 r = -0.383
 9. Death by injury, F 5-14                                   r = -0.351
10. School enrollment, primary, gender parity index           r = -0.350
26 of 128 predictors have p < 0.05 (uncorrected).

PREDICTIVE PERFORMANCE (random forest, top-15 |r| screening inside training folds):
- Repeated 10 x 5-fold CV, out-of-fold R² = 0.38 (range 0.28-0.49)
- Leave-one-out CV R² = 0.38
- Nested 5 x 5 CV R² = 0.29
- Ridge regression benchmark R² = 0.29
- Single 70/30 splits: R² ranges from -0.36 to 0.78 depending on the split
Most of the cross-country variation in the income trend is not explained.

VARIABLE IMPORTANCE:
- Share of deaths by injury among women aged 15-59 is selected in 100% of
  folds and is the top predictor by both impurity-based importance (40%)
  and permutation importance on held-out folds (0.43).

STRUCTURE (96 rate indicators; 32 count indicators excluded):
- PCA: PC1 15.6%, PC2 9.7% of variance
- k-means silhouette 0.13-0.14 for k = 2-6: no sharply separated groups
- k = 2: clusters of 21 and 41 countries, median GDP trend 40.0 vs 16.3
  (Mann-Whitney p = 0.006); bootstrap ARI 0.29, so membership is unstable
- With all 128 indicators, k = 2 isolates the 6 most populous countries
  (PC1 is a population-size factor)
